**Sampling fishnet detail.**

The main panel zooms into an 8 by 8 block of the sampling fishnet in the western Great Lakes study
area (EPSG:5070, CONUS Albers equal-area). Each grid cell is 112 pixels of 30 m Landsat resolution
to a side, that is 3,360 m, as marked by the dimension arrow on the outlined cell and the label
below the grid. Cells are labeled with their unique grid id; the five cells filled in dark gray are
interpreted reference cells drawn within this block, rendered semi-transparent so the landscape
shows through. A shaded-relief basemap under the grid shows the underlying terrain, lakes, and
rivers. The locator inset shows the whole study area with the full fishnet, and the red rectangle
marks the zoom window. A north arrow and a segmented scale bar in kilometers are included, matching
the study-area figure. Basemap: Esri World Shaded Relief.

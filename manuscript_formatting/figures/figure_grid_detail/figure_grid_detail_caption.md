**Sampling fishnet detail.**

The main panel zooms into an 8 by 8 block of the sampling fishnet in the western Great Lakes study
area (EPSG:5070, CONUS Albers equal-area). Each grid cell is 112 pixels of 30 m Landsat resolution
to a side, that is 3,360 m, as marked by the dimension arrow on the outlined cell. Cells are
labeled with their unique grid id; the five cells filled in dark gray are interpreted reference
cells drawn within this block. The locator inset shows the whole study area with the full fishnet,
and the red rectangle marks the zoom window. A north arrow and a segmented scale bar in kilometers
are included, matching the study-area figure.
